MANE
bio · gallery · instagram · bandcamp · contact

Helsinki-based, Berlin-forged and Mexican born. Yearly guest of Mexico's beloved Sunday Sunday, former resident of Berlin's Pantheon Bar collective and today Helsinki's upcoming selector.

As a young resident of Mexico's club Aura, he opened for artists like Matias Aguayo, Magda, Stephan Bodzin, Andre Kraml & many more. Becoming a proper reference in the Mexican house music scene and forging a deep-running connection to Berlin's underground soon after.

Part of Berlin's nightlife fabric as a resident of The Pantheon Bar Collective, he was behind the decks in illegal abandoned and unconventional spaces with a Funktion-One rig and a pair of 1200s, from the top of Teufelsberg to the bridge where Else would later emerge. His Berlin years saw sets at Sisyphos, Farbfernseher, Paloma Bar, Prince Charles, Rummelsbucht, Brunnen70 & more, alongside a residency at Bar Mysliwska.

In Helsinki, he's found a new home without skipping the rills. Appearances at strongholds like Stidila and Tavastia sit alongside a steady presence in the city's smaller rooms and bars — co-running an Acid House night at Bar Tÿpo or playing soulful sets just to listen to.

Releases through mexican Short Attention Records and Berlin's KK label, keep him real from the studio to the dancefloor. A Detroit ethos stalwart: soul over ego, feeling over perfection. Music as a spiritual thing, always in search of that moment when the mix makes the room feel like one.
