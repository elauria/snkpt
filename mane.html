About

I come from the Mayan's Snake Pit. A sacred paradise turned GTA metaverse. It's been always full of snakes and it will always be. Anyways, at 17 I used to throw parties with friends to play my own music, made with Fruity Loops v3.0 and played on rented Denon CDJs.[1]

Around 2007 I played my first public gig in a party with Matias Aguayo. After that I got a residency at Club Aura, where I could open/close nights for many international artists and play for 4 digit people.[2]

By 2010 I moved to Berlin and got my first turntables[3]. Lucky to end up living near Hardwax (when it was at Maybach-Ufer), even though I didn't buy many records there[4]. Soon after I joined The Pantheon Bar collective and got to play in abandoned places, like on the top of Teufelsberg, under the Elsenbrücke (where Else is located now), and some other misplaced locations. Those times will be forever in my heart; The TPB crew was special. I was also blessed to play in clubs like Sisyphos, Farbfernsher, Paloma Bar, Rummelsbucht, Brunnen 70s, etc.. and to have a bi-monthly residency at Bar Mysliwska with my friend Rimini.

Since 2020 I'm based in Helsinki. I have way more time to focus on my own stuff and the scene here although small is like a cauldron of talent. Seriously underrated!

I have released a couple of records with Short Attention and I'm looking forward to continue releasing on my own. Also every year I go to MX, to play at beloved SundaySunday and the small bars in the big city.

cheers

- Mane
