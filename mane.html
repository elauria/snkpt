Helsinki-based, Berlin-raised and Mexican born. Yearly guest of Mexico's beloved Sunday Sunday, former resident of Berlin's Pantheon Bar Collective and today's Helsinki nightlife.

As a young resident of Mexico's Club Aura, he opened for names like Matias Aguayo, Magda, Stephan Bodzin, Andre Kraml & many more. Becoming a proper reference in the Mexican electronic scene, forged a deep-running connection to Berlin's underground soon after.

Part of Berlin's nightlife fabric as a resident of The Pantheon Bar Collective, he was behind the decks in abandoned and unconventional spaces with a Funktion-One rig and a pair of 1200s, from the top of Teufelsberg to the bridges where Else would later emerge. His Berlin years saw sets at Sisyphos, Farbfernseher, Prince Charles, Rummelsbucht & more, alongside a residency at Bar Mysliwska.

In Helsinki, he's found a new home without skipping the rills. Appearances at Stidila and Tavastia sit alongside a steady presence in the city's smaller rooms and bars — co-running an Acid House night at Bar Tÿpo or playing soulful sets to listen to, he still carries the instinct that shaped him in Berlin.

Releases through Mexico's Short Attention Records and Berlin's KK label, keep him real from the studio to the dancefloor. A Detroit ethos stalwart: soul over ego, feeling over perfection. Music as a spiritual thing, always in search of that moment when the mix brings the room into one.
