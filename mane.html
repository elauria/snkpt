Helsinki-based, Berlin-proofed and Mexican born. Yearly guest of Mexico's beloved SundaySunday, former member of The Pantheon Bar and today's Helsinki upcoming selector.

I can't stand pretending this was written by somebody else and even less something written by A-fucking-I. I'm gonna beat the fucking Algos. This is my story:

I was born in the Mexican caribe and proud. My first public gig was opening for Matias Aguayo. Later on I had a residency at Club Aura where I warmed up and closed nights for artists like Magda, Stephan Bodzin, Andre Kraml and many more. Fun times even when playing for a full club of 1000 ppl was scary as hell.

By 2010 I said fuck it and moved to Berlin. I ended up living in front of Hardwax (when it was at Maybachufer). I didn't buy many records there though (One day I asked for Omar-S music and they didn't know it so I never trusted them so much again). Eventually I joined The Pantheon Bar collective (who used to bring Funktion One sound systems to abandoned buildings) and ended up playing at their parties. I can't forget the one on top of Teufelsberg (the former NSA telecom spy headquarters built on top of WW2 debris). That place was magical and so it was the night. It's not something I can describe, but at least the set was recorded and you can listen to it here.

I was also lucky to play at some of the best clubs there: Sisyphos, Prince Charles, Farbfernseher (a favorite of mine), Paloma Bar, Rummelsbucht, Brunnen70, etc. alongside a residency at Bar Mysliwska... In case this information is exiting for you as it was for me (doubt it).

In 2020, I moved to Helsinki. It's beautiful here and the scene although small, is very talented and passionate. It's an ideal place to grow as a musician, I think. I spend most of my days in the studio making music, and playing out at clubs or bars. Stidilä was a highlight last year, an after hours that sells no alcohol; the closest I've felt to Berlin.

Production-wise I have a couple of releases with Short Attention Records (a mexican label with chicago and detroit connections), and I'm looking forward to continue releasing on my own.

I like to think I follow the Detroit ethos: Soul over egos, vibe over perfection. Music as a spiritual thing. Always in search for the moment when the mix makes the room feel like one.

cheers

- Mane
